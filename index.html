    FAQ (nobody asked)

    Are these real gang signs?

    No. These are universally known gestures — peace sign, rock horns, shaka, thumbs up, the OK circle, Spock's salute, the Italian pinch, crossed fingers, ASL "I love you" — wearing silly fake-gang costumes. The lore is satire. The hand shapes are just hand shapes.

    Will this get me in trouble?

    The most dangerous thing on this site is the Circle Game below-the-waist rule, and even that only obligates a friendly bop. Go be nice to people.

    Wait, is the ILY sign making fun of sign language?

    Opposite — 🤟 is real American Sign Language for "I love you", and it's included with respect. ASL is a full language with its own grammar; this site is a joke about internet meme culture, not about ASL. Consider actually learning ASL. It's a superpower.

    Why does the tracker keep misreading my thumb?

    Hand landmarks are 21 dots estimated from a tiny webcam image; thumbs are drama queens. Good light, hand fully in frame, palm facing the camera. If it still fails — the tracker is sassy. Try again.

    Does my video get uploaded anywhere?

    No. Frames are processed locally by MediaPipe in your browser and never leave your device. There is no server, no upload, no storage. Your hands remain between you and your webcam.

    Can we add our own crew's signs?

    Yes — that's the whole point. See docs/ADD-A-SIGN.md: each sign is a few lines of data (finger states + steps + lore). Teach the dev, ship the sign.
